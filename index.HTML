Village Water Problem

THE PROBLEM

Many rural families struggle with water quality.

In our village, families depend on existing water sources for drinking and household use. Community members have concerns about water hardness and drinking-water quality.

  • ✓ Concerns about drinking-water quality
  • ✓ Hard water affecting the community
  • ✓ Limited access to affordable treated water
  • ✓ Need for regular water-quality monitoring
OUR SOLUTION

A Community Water Treatment Plant

We want to establish a local water-treatment system designed according to the actual water quality of the village.

01

Test

Professionally test the existing water source.

02

Treat

Install the appropriate purification system.

03

Monitor

Regularly monitor water quality and plant performance.

04

Serve

Provide treated drinking water to the community.
